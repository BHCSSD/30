```python
def calculate_discount(customer):
    if customer is not None:
        if "membership" in customer:
            if customer["membership"] == "premium":
                if customer["purchase_total"] > 100:
                    print("Discount: 20%")
                else:
                    print("Discount: 10%")
            else:
                print("No discount for non-premium members")
        else:
            print("Customer membership info missing")
    else:
        print("Customer data missing")

```

> [!TIP]
> **Guard Clause Principle:** Check for failure conditions or prerequisites at the beginning of a function and exit immediately (using `return`) if they are not met. This leaves the "happy path" of your logic at the lowest indentation level.
