* **The Warp Drive:** A **Spaceship** (Manager) in the center; as it "accelerates," **Stars** (Components) fly from the center toward the edges, increasing in speed.

## **Technical Constraints**

### **Class 1: The Component (The Child Class)**
* **Properties:** Must have `x`, `y`, and a `life` or `alpha/size` property.
* **Method `update()`:** Must change the internal state (move position, reduce life, or change size).
* **Method `display()`:** Draws the component. 
* **Creative Requirement:** Use at least one variable to change its appearance over time (it shrinks or changes color as it "ages").

### **Class 2: The Manager (The Parent Class)**
* **The Container:** Must initialize an **empty array** in the constructor.
* **The Factory:** A method like `spawn()` that uses the `new` keyword to create components.
* **The Delegation Loop:** A method that loops through the array and tells every component to `update()` and `display()`.
* **The Janitor (Memory Management):** You **must** use `.filter()` to remove components from the array once their "death condition" is met.
